How to find us

Families and first-timers

Never ridden off-road? No problem. We have children's bikes in a range of sizes, a tag-along and double trailers for little ones, and Nigel will suggest a gentle route, like the lake loop or Lôn Eifion.

Groups, big and small

From a family get-together to a stag do, your bikes are ready when you arrive, and e-bikes help everyone keep up. Our trailer carries 15 bikes, so we can bring a whole group's bikes to you.

The Quarry route, in Lonely Planet

Llwybr y Chwarel

Our Quarry route is in Lonely Planet's Great Britain guide, and riders come from all over the world to ride it.

It climbs through the old Dinorwig slate quarry, part of the Slate Landscape of Northwest Wales World Heritage Site, with Llyn Padarn below and the mountains of Eryri all around. Every hire comes with the route map, and Nigel will talk you through it before you set off.

  • Starts in the village
  • Route map with every hire

Meet Nigel and the family

Amdanom ni

We're a family business. Nigel and his family swapped the flat lands of Norfolk for the mountains of North Wales in 2001, and we've been hiring bikes in Llanberis for around 15 years. Nigel has toured Britain and Europe by bike, and has been mountain biking since 2004.

Lots of our riders have never hired a bike before, and they come from all over the world. Nigel will take the time to get you on the right bike and the right route. You'll often find him out on the local trails with his spaniels, Ash and one-eyed Luna.

Hire terms

Telerau llogi

The short version, in plain English.

Opening, booking and deposits

  • Open every day, 10am to 5pm. Evening hire by arrangement.
  • Booking isn't always needed, but it's a good idea at busy times.
  • When you collect, you leave a deposit: a card, cash, driving licence, passport or car keys.
  • Discounts for groups, regular riders and hires of two days or more.
  • Plans changed? Let us know as early as you can.

Riding safely

  • Everyone in your group needs to be fit and able to ride.
  • We check every bike before it goes out. You're responsible for riding safely and within your ability.
  • Our insurance only covers you if you wear the helmets we provide, and lock the bikes to something secure whenever you leave them.
  • Snowdon: no cycling on the bridleways from 10am to 5pm, 1 May to 30 September. More about the agreement

Your hire agreement

  • When you collect your bikes, you sign a short hire agreement.
  • It covers looking after the bikes and helmets, and what happens if something is damaged or lost.

Your details

  • We only use your details to look after your hire and to reply to you.
  • We never sell them or pass them on for marketing. Read our privacy notice
