FLôn Las Ogwen: Bangor to Llyn Ogwen

ModerateGrade
17.6 km10.9 miles
359 mof climbing
2 to 3 hoursBangor to Llyn Ogwen, with drop-off and pick-up

From the sea at Bangor up into the mountains: old railway paths through the woods, past the Penrhyn slate quarry, and up the Nant Ffrancon valley to Llyn Ogwen, under Tryfan.

The route

  1. Nigel drops you and the bikes at Porth Penrhyn, the old slate harbour on the edge of Bangor.
  2. Follow the path inland on the old quarry railway, through the woods of the Cegin valley.
  3. At Tregarth, follow the signs through the village. There is about 1.3 km on the B4409 towards Bethesda: take care.
  4. Rejoin the path past the edge of Penrhyn Quarry, with the mountains ahead, and follow the Afon Ogwen beyond Bethesda.
  5. Join the quiet old road up the Nant Ffrancon valley.
  6. The last climb to Ogwen is steep. Finish at Llyn Ogwen, where Nigel collects you and the bikes.

Good to know

  • About two thirds is traffic-free. The rest is on quiet roads, apart from the short stretch of the B4409.
  • It climbs all the way from the sea to about 300 m, gently at first, with a steep finish. Ridden the other way, from Ogwen to Bangor, it is mostly downhill.
  • For a shorter, gentler ride, stop at Tregarth (about 6 km).
  • There is a snack bar and toilets at Ogwen.
