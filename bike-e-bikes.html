Get to know your e-bike

New to e-bikes? Tap a dot to see what each part does.

1 2 3 4 5 6

  1. The display on the left of the handlebar switches the motor on and sets how much help you get. Start on a low setting and the battery lasts longer. The gear lever is on the right.

  2. Clips onto the frame and comes off to charge. A full charge is good for about 20 to 30 miles, depending on the hills and how much help you use.

  3. Sits in the back wheel. It only helps while you're pedalling, and eases off at 15.5 mph, the UK limit for e-bikes.

  4. Disc brakes front and back, which work well in the wet. Use both levers together, gently. Nigel will show you which lever is which before you set off.

  5. 100 mm of travel in the fork takes the edge off rocks, roots and bumpy tracks.

  6. Nigel sets the height for each rider. With a pedal at the bottom, your leg should be almost straight.
