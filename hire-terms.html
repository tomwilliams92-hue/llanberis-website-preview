Hire terms

Telerau llogi

The terms for hiring bikes and kit from us, in plain English. When we say "we" or "us", we mean Llanberis Bike Hire Limited. "You" means the person who books and anyone riding with them.

Booking

  • You can book by phone, by text, by sending us a message, or by turning up when we have bikes free.
  • Booking isn't always needed, but it's a good idea at busy times, especially in the school holidays.
  • Your booking is confirmed when Nigel confirms it with you.
  • When you book, tell us each rider's height and riding experience, so we can have the right bikes ready and suggest a route that suits everyone.

Prices and paying

  • Our prices are on our bikes and prices section. Ask us for e-bike prices, and about delivery.
  • There are discounts for groups, regular riders and hires of two or more days in a row.
  • You pay for your hire in full, by card, cash or bank transfer. We'll tell you when payment is due when we confirm your booking.

Deposits

  • When you collect your bikes, you leave a deposit: a card, cash, driving licence, passport or car keys.
  • We give it back when the bikes and kit come back to us.

Changing or cancelling

  • Plans changed, or the weather's turned? Tell us as soon as you can.
  • We'll always try to move your hire to another day first. If that doesn't work for you, talk to us about a refund.

Collecting and returning

  • Collect your bikes from the yard behind 34 High Street, Llanberis, or we can deliver them to where you're staying or a drop-off point.
  • When you collect, you sign a short hire agreement.
  • Please bring the bikes back by the time we agreed. If you're running late, call or text us on 07776 051559.

Riding safely

  • Everyone in your group needs to be fit and able to ride, and to ride within their ability.
  • We check every bike before it goes out. If something doesn't feel right, stop and call us.
  • Wear the helmets we provide. Our insurance only covers you if you wear them, and lock the bikes to something secure whenever you leave them.
  • Only ride where cycling is allowed: bridleways, forest tracks, cycle paths and roads, never footpaths.
  • On Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa), please keep to the voluntary agreement: from 1 May to 30 September, no cycling on the bridleways to the summit between 10am and 5pm. Read the agreement on the Eryri National Park website.

Looking after the bikes

  • Look after the bikes and kit while they're with you.
  • If anything is damaged, lost or stolen while it's with you, we may charge for the repair or replacement. We'll always talk it through with you first.
  • If you have a breakdown or a puncture you can't fix, call us and we'll help. If we need to come out and collect you, there may be a charge.

Our responsibility

We take care to give you safe, well-maintained bikes and good advice. You ride at your own risk and are responsible for riding safely. Nothing in these terms limits our responsibility where the law doesn't allow it, such as for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or takes away your rights as a consumer.

Your details

We only use your details to look after your hire and to reply to you. Our privacy notice explains more.
