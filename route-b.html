BRound Llyn Padarn and the Dinorwig quarries

The route

  1. From the yard, follow the lakeside cycle path through the tunnel to the end of Llyn Padarn.
  2. At Brynrefail, head up through Clwt-y-bont to Deiniolen.
  3. Climb through Dinorwig and into the quarries at "bus stop quarry".
  4. Ride through the quarries past Dali's Hole.
  5. Come down the zig-zags of "Watford Gap" to the bottom of Llyn Peris.
  6. Head back to Llanberis alongside Llyn Peris.

Good to know

  • Not suitable for trailers or tag-alongs.
  • Food: the Caban café in Brynrefail, and the Vaynol Arms in Nant Peris.
  • Some steep, loose quarry tracks: take it steady on the way down.
