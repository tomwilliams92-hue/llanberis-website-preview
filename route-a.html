AClegyr Road circular

EasyGrade

A gentle loop along Llyn Padarn and back over Clegyr Road, with a long freewheel into Llanberis and views of Snowdon all the way.

The route

  1. From the yard, join the lakeside cycle path along Llyn Padarn.
  2. Go through the old railway tunnel and carry on to the end of the lake.
  3. Turn almost immediately left by the skate park, into Cwm-y-glo.
  4. Turn left into Ffordd y Bryn, opposite the school, and follow the road past Bryn Bras Castle on your left.
  5. At the main road turn left, under the arch of Bryn Bras Castle, and climb steeply past Bryn Teg caravan park.
  6. Turn left onto Clegyr Road and keep going up. Cross the cattle grid, then freewheel all the way back to Llanberis.

Good to know

  • Fine with a trailer or tag-along this way round.
  • Without a trailer, there is a shortcut up Allt Goch and a quiet footpath by a stream (push your bike on the footpath).
  • Take care on the short stretch of main road.
  • The Caban café in Brynrefail is close to the end of the lake.
