Privacy notice

Hysbysiad preifatrwydd

How we look after the details you send us through this website.

Who we are

Llanberis Bike Hire Limited (company number 07231295) runs this website and is responsible for your details. You can reach us on 07776 051559 or at llanberisbikehire@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send us a message through the form on this website, we receive:

  • your name
  • your email address or mobile number
  • anything else you choose to tell us: the day, how many riders, what kind of riding and your message.

If you call, text or email us instead, we receive the details you give us that way.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and to arrange your hire. The law lets us do this because you've asked us to, and because it's needed before we can take your booking.

We don't use your details for marketing, and we never sell them or pass them on to anyone else to use.

Who else sees it

  • Your message comes to us by email. Our email is provided by Google, which may store it outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires.
  • Our website is hosted by Krystal Hosting Ltd in the UK. Like any web host, it keeps short-term records of visits (such as your device's internet address) to keep the site secure.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry, and no longer than 12 months. If you go on to hire from us, we keep the booking records we need for our accounts and insurance.

Cookies and maps

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Our route pages show maps from OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap. To show the map, your browser asks their servers for the map pictures, which means they see your device's internet address, as any website you visit would.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. Just call or email us.

If you're unhappy with how we've used your details, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.
