DYr Wyddfa (Snowdon) by the Llanberis Path

The big one: up the Llanberis Path to the summit of Yr Wyddfa, at 1,085 m the highest point in Wales, and back down the same way.

The route

  1. From the yard, head south through the village to the start of the Llanberis Path, on Victoria Terrace by the Royal Victoria Hotel.
  2. Climb the very steep lane to the gate where the path itself begins.
  3. Follow the wide stony path up the mountain, alongside the Snowdon Mountain Railway, past Halfway House.
  4. Go under the railway near Clogwyn station, then up the steepest part of the climb to Bwlch Glas, where the paths meet.
  5. Follow the path beside the railway to the top. Walk the last few metres to the summit.
  6. Come back down the same way. Keep your speed down: it is rocky, and busy with walkers.

Good to know

  • From 1 May to 30 September, please don't ride any part of the Llanberis Path between 10am and 5pm. This is the Yr Wyddfa voluntary cycling agreement, run by Eryri National Park. It doesn't apply from 1 October to 30 April.
  • Never ride on the railway line. It is illegal and dangerous.
  • Walkers and horse riders have right of way on a bridleway. Slow down and say hello.
  • Mountain weather changes fast and the top is often in cloud. Take waterproofs, warm layers, food, water and a map.
  • In season there is a café at Halfway House, and the summit café at Hafod Eryri opens when the trains run to the top.
