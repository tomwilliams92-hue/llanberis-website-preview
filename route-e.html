ELôn Eifion: Caernarfon to Bryncir

Very easyGrade
19.7 km12.2 miles
146 mof climbing
2 to 3 hoursCaernarfon to Bryncir, with drop-off and pick-up

A gentle, traffic-free ride on the old railway line from Caernarfon, with views of Yr Eifl and the Snowdon range. Finish at Penygroes, or carry on to Bryncir.

The route

  1. Nigel drops you and the bikes in Caernarfon, at the start of Lôn Eifion by the Welsh Highland Railway station.
  2. Follow the path south, beside the Welsh Highland Railway, as far as Dinas.
  3. Carry on along the old railway line through Llanwnda and Groeslon.
  4. At Penygroes, about 10 km in, the path passes the village: a good place for a break, or to finish if you're doing the shorter ride.
  5. Carry on south past Pant Glas to the end of the path at Bryncir.
  6. Nigel collects you and the bikes at the end.

Good to know

  • Traffic-free nearly all the way, and almost flat.
  • For a shorter ride, finish at Penygroes: about 10 km, 1 to 2 hours.
  • Tarmac and firm, smooth path: fine for children's bikes, trailers and tag-alongs.
  • Shared with walkers. Ring your bell and pass slowly.
  • Lôn Eifion is part of National Cycle Network route 8. In Caernarfon it joins Lôn Las Menai to Y Felinheli.
